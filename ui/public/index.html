Clone a voice (Hindi)

Upload 5 to 12 seconds of one person speaking Hindi clearly, with no music (a Voice Memos recording is fine), and type exactly what they say in Devanagari. A wrong text makes the clone mumble. Press the play button on the new voice to hear it; the first play takes about 20 seconds.
