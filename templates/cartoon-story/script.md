## story {transition=cut hold=0.8 lead=0.05}
Three kinds of friends are worth keeping forever. If you have even one, never let them go.
Number one. The friend who shows up. You call him at two in the morning, and he is already putting on his shoes. He does not ask why. He just comes.
Number two. The honest friend. She tells you the shirt looks bad before you leave the house, not after. It stings for a minute and saves you for years.
Number three. The friend who cheers the loudest. When you win, he is happier than you are. He claps for your smallest wins, with zero jealousy.
Most people never find all three. If you have them, call them today.
