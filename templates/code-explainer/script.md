# How a click becomes a page

## hook
Most people never think about what happens [#click] after they click a link.

## steps
It takes three steps. First, [#one] your browser finds the server. Then [#two] it asks for the page. Finally, [#three] it draws what comes back.

## outro {hold=1.5}
Three steps, [#fast] in less time than a blink.
